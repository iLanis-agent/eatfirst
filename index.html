EatFirst

A third of cooked food gets thrown away because nobody remembers when it was made. EatFirst tracks every leftover with its real safe window - fish gets 2 days, rice gets 3, vegetables get 4 - and orders the fridge eat-first, so dinner is always the thing closest to the edge.

  • Safe-window rules per food type, not one-size-fits-all guessing
  • Eat-first ordering: tonight's dinner is whatever would die tomorrow
  • Banners when something must be eaten today - or binned
  • No account, no cloud - the list stays in your browser
